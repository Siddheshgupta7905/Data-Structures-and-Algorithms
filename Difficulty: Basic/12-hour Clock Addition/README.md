<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/12-hour-clock-addition1206/1">12-hour Clock Addition</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Basic</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Given two integers <strong>x </strong></span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">and</span>&nbsp;<strong><span style="font-size: 18.6667px;">y</span></strong><span style="font-size: 14pt;">, where x represents an hour on a 12-hour clock, determine the hour reached after moving forward y </span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">hours. </span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">A 12-hour clock follows the sequence:&nbsp; </span><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><span style="font-size: 18.6667px;">1 -&gt; 2 -&gt; 3 -&gt; ... -&gt; 12 -&gt; 1 -&gt; 2. </span></span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Return the resulting hour after the movement.</span></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">Examples:</span></strong></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong style="font-size: 18px;">Input:</strong><span style="font-size: 18px;"> <span style="font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, 'Open Sans', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif; font-size: 18.6667px; white-space: normal;">x </span>= 5, </span><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, Open Sans, Helvetica Neue, sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 18.6667px; white-space-collapse: collapse;">y </span></span><span style="font-size: 18px;">= 7
</span></span><strong style="font-size: 18px;">Output:</strong><span style="font-size: 18px;"> 12
</span><strong style="font-size: 18px;">Explaination:</strong><span style="font-size: 18px;"> Starting from hour 5 and moving forward 7 hours: 5 -&gt; 6 -&gt; 7 -&gt; 8 -&gt; 9 -&gt; 10 -&gt; 11 -&gt; 12. </span></span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Hence, the resulting hour is <span style="font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, 'Open Sans', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif;">12</span><span style="font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, 'Open Sans', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif;">.</span></span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input:</strong> x<span style="font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, 'Open Sans', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif; font-size: 18.6667px; white-space: normal;"> </span>= 10, y = 5<br><strong>Output:</strong> 3
<strong>Explaination:</strong> Starting from hour 10 and moving forward 5 hours: 10 -&gt; 11 -&gt; 12 -&gt; 1 -&gt; 2 -&gt; 3. Hence, the resulting hour is 3.</span></pre>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Constraints:</strong><br></span><span style="font-size: 18px;">1 ≤ x ≤ 12<br></span><span style="font-size: 18px;">0 ≤ y </span><span style="font-size: 18px;">≤ 10</span><sup>9</sup></p></div><br><p><span style=font-size:18px><strong>Topic Tags : </strong><br><code>Mathematics</code>&nbsp;<code>Modular Arithmetic</code>&nbsp;
